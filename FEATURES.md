# Context Window — Features & Use Cases

> The invisible company brain. It quietly reads the tools your team already uses,
> builds a living memory that stays current, and **guides + alerts** everyone — in the
> tools they already work in. Powerful underneath, effortless on top.

---

## The core capabilities (the engine everything is built on)

- **Lifetime institutional memory** — once something is known (a PR merged, a decision made, a price agreed, a "feature X is done" message from an engineer), it's remembered forever and answered instantly the next time anyone asks.
- **Stays current (the moat)** — when a fact changes it supersedes the old one; when it goes stale it's forgotten. You're never quoted yesterday's truth.
- **Escalation → learning loop** — if the brain isn't sure, it routes the question to the *right* person (found via their PRs / their work), gets the answer, replies, and remembers it so no one is ever interrupted for the same thing twice.
- **Code-aware** — it can see the codebase, so it knows what's *actually shipped* vs. what's only on a branch / in dev ("the dev has this feature, but main doesn't yet").
- **Provenance on every answer** — every reply ships with its sources, so anyone can verify in one click.
- **Role- & permission-aware** — answers are tailored to who's asking (a CEO, a CTO, a sales rep) and limited to what that person is allowed to see.
- **Proactive, not passive** — daily "today" briefings, deadline reminders, and risk/issue flags pushed to the right people. It guides and warns; it doesn't sit in a dashboard waiting.
- **Research agent** — runs deep research on internal data *and* the web, scoped to the asker's role and intent.
- **Lives where you work** — Slack, Claude/MCP, CRM, email, Drive, GitHub and more. No new app to adopt, zero learning curve.

## By team — what it does for each role

### Founders / CEO
- A morning briefing: what moved yesterday, what's at risk, what needs you.
- "What did we decide about X?" — instant recall of decisions and the context behind them.
- Role-scoped research on anything (a market, a strategy, a competitor) using internal data + the web.
- Drafts board/investor updates and all-hands notes from what actually happened.
- Surfaces silent risks: a delayed hire, a slipping deadline, a customer about to churn.

### CTO / Engineering
- **Architecture & design help** — with full company context, designing the right system (and AI features) becomes easy; it proposes options grounded in your real stack and constraints.
- **Technical research** — the CTO asks it to research a library, approach, or trade-off and it returns a grounded answer because it already has the data.
- Code-aware answers: what's shipped, what's in review, who owns what (via PRs).
- Onboarding ramp for new engineers — "where is X", "why did we do Y this way", instantly.
- Remembers incidents & postmortems; flags recurring issues and tech debt.
- Auto-drafts release notes / changelogs from merged work.

### Sales
- **Demo guidance** — when a rep is stuck on a feature's flow ("where do I show what?"), it walks them through it, knowing what's actually live vs. in-dev.
- **Collateral on demand** — shares the right pricing, deck, or one-pager (sourced from what the founders produced).
- **Past & current features** — answers what we've shipped and what's available now, so reps never oversell or undersell.
- **Follow-up suggestions & email drafting** — drafts the next touch tailored to the deal's context and history.
- **Objection handling & battlecards** — pulls the best past answers and competitive positioning.
- **Deal memory** — what was promised to which customer, and by whom.
- **(Future) AI sales teammate** — with full context it can join meetings, run demos, and answer prospects live.

### Customer Success / Support
- "When will feature X be done?" — answered from engineering's real status (PRs / a quick note from the tech team), remembered for everyone after.
- Answers tickets from past resolutions; routes the hard ones to the right engineer automatically.
- Flags churn-risk and escalation signals early.
- Preps QBRs and renewal conversations with the full account history.

### Product / PM
- Aggregates feature requests across Slack, tickets, and calls into one place.
- "What did customers say about X?" with quotes and sources.
- Keeps a living decision log and roadmap status; drafts specs from discussion.

### Marketing
- **Competitive analysis** — ongoing positioning vs. competitors, refreshed as things change.
- Keeps messaging consistent; repurposes past content and mines customer quotes for case studies.
- Coordinates launches across teams so nothing is missed.

### People / HR & Ops
- Onboarding: instant answers to "where's the doc", "who owns this", "what's the policy".
- **Flags delays & issues in the system** — e.g., a new hire's start date slipping, an overdue task, a process bottleneck.
- **Knowledge retention** — when someone leaves, their context stays in the brain instead of walking out the door.

### Across everyone — email & calendar
- Reads inboxes, surfaces what matters, and drafts replies for approval.
- Creates calendar reminders and nudges before deadlines.
- Points out issues proactively (delays, blockers, things falling through the cracks).

## More places this can be used (expansion ideas)

1. **Onboarding any new hire** in any role — they get a knowledgeable teammate from day one.
2. **Meeting intelligence** — auto-notes, decisions, and action items from every meeting; "what did we agree?" later.
3. **Standup / status auto-summaries** — no more manual updates; the brain knows.
4. **Investor / board prep** — pulls metrics, wins, and risks into a draft.
5. **Hiring & recruiting** — candidate status, interview feedback recall, "did the offer get accepted?".
6. **Finance & procurement** — vendor/contract memory, renewal reminders, "what did we agree to pay?".
7. **Legal & compliance** — where's the DPA, what's in the contract, audit-ready trails.
8. **Incident response** — pulls runbooks, past incidents, and the right on-call owner instantly.
9. **Partnerships / BD** — remembers every partner conversation and commitment.
10. **Internal helpdesk** — IT/HR/ops questions answered without pinging a human.
11. **Pre-meeting briefs** — who's in the room, history with them, open items, before every call.
12. **Quarterly / annual reviews** — assembles what each person/team actually shipped.
13. **Pricing & quoting** — generate accurate quotes from current pricing and deal context.
14. **Roadmap & release comms** — keep CS, sales, and customers in sync on what's shipping.
15. **Risk & deadline radar** — continuously watches for slips, blockers, and silent failures.
16. **Scheduled research reports** — recurring competitive / market briefs delivered automatically.
17. **(Future) Autonomous agents** — draft-and-send follow-ups, auto-update the CRM, attend & demo in meetings, run research end-to-end.
